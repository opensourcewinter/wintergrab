# Code of conduct

wintergrab follows the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It
applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever
someone speaks for the project.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and kind, to newcomers most of all.
- Assume good faith. Criticize ideas and code, never people.
- Say plainly what is wrong, and help find what would be right.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks;
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention;
- publishing others' private information (an address, an email,
  credentials) without their permission;
- anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

If something happens that should not, report it privately to the
maintainers. Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull
request, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are
read by the maintainers only, and handled confidentially.

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this
code, and may warn, suspend or ban those who break it, as the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
